# Clinical Reference Guide: Cardiovascular Disease and Coronary Artery Risk

## 1. Overview & Disease Description
Cardiovascular Disease (CVD), encompassing Coronary Artery Disease (CAD), Atherosclerotic Cardiovascular Disease (ASCVD), and myocardial ischemia, is the leading cause of morbidity and mortality worldwide. CAD occurs when the coronary arteries supplying oxygen-rich blood to the myocardium become narrowed or occluded by atherosclerotic plaques.

### Major Clinical Manifestations:
- **Angina Pectoris**: Substernal chest discomfort provoked by physical exertion or emotional stress and relieved by rest or nitroglycerin.
- **Myocardial Infarction (MI)**: Irreversible necrosis of heart muscle secondary to prolonged acute ischemia.
- **Heart Failure**: Impaired ventricular filling or ejection fraction resulting from chronic ischemic damage.

## 2. Key Clinical Biomarkers & Risk Factors

### Chest Pain Types (CP Classification)
- **Typical Angina (Substernal Chest Pain)**: Meets 3 criteria: substernal discomfort with characteristic quality and duration, provoked by exertion/emotional stress, relieved by rest/nitroglycerin within minutes. Highest predictive value for obstructive CAD.
- **Atypical Angina**: Meets 2 of the typical angina criteria. Frequently observed in women, elderly patients, and individuals with diabetes.
- **Non-Anginal Chest Pain**: Meets $\le 1$ criterion. Musculoskeletal, gastrointestinal (reflux), or anxiety-related discomfort.
- **Asymptomatic / Silent Ischemia**: Ischemia without typical symptoms, prevalent in diabetic neuropathy.

### Resting Blood Pressure (trestbps)
- **Normal**: < 120/80 mmHg.
- **Stage 1 Hypertension**: Systolic 130–139 mmHg or Diastolic 80–89 mmHg.
- **Stage 2 Hypertension**: Systolic >= 140 mmHg or Diastolic >= 90 mmHg.
- **Pathophysiology**: Chronic hemodynamic strain induces arterial remodeling, endothelial shear stress, and left ventricular hypertrophy (LVH).

### Serum Cholesterol (chol)
- **Total Cholesterol**: Desirable < 200 mg/dL; Borderline high 200–239 mg/dL; High >= 240 mg/dL.
- **Atherogenic Mechanism**: Excess circulating LDL particles penetrate arterial intima, undergo oxidative modification, and provoke foam cell macrophage accumulation, driving atherosclerotic plaque formation.

### Exercise-Induced ST Depression (oldpeak) & ST-T Wave Changes
- **Oldpeak (ST Depression)**: ST-segment depression measured in millimeters on ECG during treadmill exercise testing relative to baseline rest.
- **Clinical Significance**: ST depression >= 1.0 mm signifies exercise-induced subendocardial myocardial ischemia. Values >= 2.0 mm indicate severe multivessel coronary obstruction.

### Maximum Heart Rate Achieved (thalach)
- **Target Age-Predicted Maximum**: $220 - \text{Age}$ (bpm).
- **Chronotropic Incompetence**: Failure to achieve >= 85% of age-predicted maximum heart rate during peak exercise is an independent predictor of adverse cardiovascular mortality and ischemic heart disease.

### Fasting Blood Sugar (fbs) & Resting ECG (restecg)
- Fasting blood sugar > 120 mg/dL indicates comorbid metabolic dysfunction, which multiplies vascular risk by 2- to 3-fold.
- Resting ECG abnormalities (ST-T wave inversion, left ventricular hypertrophy) indicate chronic ischemic or structural myocardial remodeling.

## 3. Prevention & Evidence-Based Lifestyle Recommendations
According to the American Heart Association (AHA) and American College of Cardiology (ACC) Guidelines:
1. **Physical Activity**:
   - 150–300 minutes per week of moderate-intensity aerobic exercise, or 75–150 minutes of vigorous aerobic activity.
2. **Cardioprotective Nutrition**:
   - Mediterranean-style or DASH diet rich in vegetables, fruits, whole grains, nuts, and lean poultry/fish.
   - Saturated fat restriction (< 6% of total caloric intake), elimination of industrial trans fats, and sodium limitation (< 2,000 mg/day).
3. **Cardiovascular Risk Reduction**:
   - Complete tobacco avoidance and avoidance of secondhand smoke.
   - Statin therapy for elevated LDL-C or high ASCVD 10-year risk estimation.
   - Regular monitoring of resting blood pressure and lipid fractions.
